VII. Budget

Project Swastik: 12-Month Budget Breakdown

The total investment for Project Swastik is $14,000 over 12 months. This budget is designed for an independent pharmacy with modest resources: no app development, no CRM software license, no marketing agency. Every line item uses existing staff, low-cost tools, and community partnerships.

Pillar 1: Drishti — Billing Transparency (Months 1–3)

Line Item Cost Justification
Copay confirmation signage & workflow materials $150 Printed workflow checklist for pharmacy counter; patient copay confirmation card (500 copies)
PA (Prior Authorization) checklist template $50 Professional template design and printing; integrated into prescription processing binder
Post-visit satisfaction survey (printed) $200 500 single-question survey cards: "How was your experience? Rate 1–5." Collected at pickup counter.
Subtotal: Drishti $400 Minimal investment; primarily printing and workflow materials

Pillar 2: Seva — Wellness Rewards Program (Months 1–8)

Line Item Cost Justification
Wellness Rewards card printing (2,000 cards) $300 High-quality loyalty card with StarCare branding; patient name and account number field; point tracker grid
In-store loyalty program signage $400 Window decal, counter card, and wall poster explaining the program; professional design and printing
Wellness points redemption inventory $2,500 Initial inventory of OTC products for point redemption: vitamins, first aid kits, thermometers, blood pressure cuffs, wellness bundles
Birthday wellness kit inventory (500 units) $750 Small wellness kits (hand sanitizer, lip balm, bandages, wellness tip card) for Sprout-tier+ member birthdays
Wellness workshop materials (4 sessions) $600 Handouts, demo products (blood pressure cuff demonstration), refreshments for each workshop
Point tracking ledger system $100 Manual point tracking ledger books for pharmacy staff; patient point tracker cards
Subtotal: Seva $4,650 Largest category: inventory for point redemption and program materials

Pillar 3: Milap — Community Connection (Months 9–12)

Line Item Cost Justification
Prosper Chamber of Commerce membership $500 Annual membership; provides networking, event access, and community credibility
Community health event supplies (4 events) $2,000 BP cuffs (disposables), A1C test kits, informational brochures, table, tent, StarCare banner for outdoor events
School sponsorship materials $1,500 Branded first aid kits for Prosper ISD events; StarCare bandanas for soccer team sponsorship; back-to-school health kit giveaway (200 units)
Community giving program (food drive bins + marketing) $300 Three donation bins for pharmacy; printed signage; small incentive stickers for Wellness Rewards members who donate
Seasonal wellness bundle inventory (holiday, spring) $3,000 Pre-assembled wellness bundles for sale and point redemption: vitamins, thermometer, first aid kit, wellness guide
Community event promotion (local ads, flyers) $1,000 Local newspaper ad (Prosper Weekly), school newsletter sponsorship, community calendar listings
Wellness workshop guest speaker honoraria $800 Small honoraria for guest speakers (nutritionist, pediatric nurse, mental health advocate) — $200 per session
Subtotal: Milap $9,100 Largest single item: seasonal wellness bundle inventory

BUDGET PHILOSOPHY Project Swastik is designed to be capital-light: no software licenses, no agency fees, no expensive technology. The largest single expense is wellness bundle inventory ($3,000), which is also a revenue-generating asset (bundles can be sold or redeemed). The budget scales with performance: as Wellness Rewards enrollment grows, point redemption costs are offset by increased prescription and OTC revenue.
