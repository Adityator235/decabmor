V. Strategic Plan: Community Connection (Milap)

Pillar 3: Milap — Community Roots (Months 9–12)

After 12 months of billing transparency (Drishti) and personalized wellness rewards (Seva), StarCare amplifies its loyalty engine with community roots — visible, tangible investments in the Prosper community that transform StarCare from a pharmacy into a community health partner.

Initiative 3.1: StarCare Community Health Program

Quarterly free health screening events hosted at StarCare Pharmacy or at Prosper community centers. Each event targets a specific health theme and is promoted through the Wellness Rewards program (attendance earns points):

Q1: Heart Health Month (February)

Free blood pressure, cholesterol, and A1C screenings. Blood pressure checks are integrated into the Wellness Rewards program (25 points per screening). Cholesterol and A1C screenings are offered at quarterly community events.

Expected attendance: 80–120 patients. New patient acquisition: 15–25 households.

Q2: Family Health Fair (May)

Pediatric health screenings, first aid certification workshop, sunscreen & first aid kit distribution for families. Partner with Prosper ISD schools and PTSA for promotion.

Expected attendance: 150–200 families. New patient acquisition: 25–40 households.

Q3: Senior Wellness Day (August)

Medication review for seniors (polypharmacy check), blood pressure monitoring, nutrition counseling. Partner with Prosper Senior Center and local assisted living facilities.

Expected attendance: 60–80 seniors. New patient acquisition: 10–15 households.

Q4: Holiday Wellness Drive (November–December)

Flu shot campaign, vitamin D supplementation advice (winter), medication disposal information. Double referral points for existing Wellness Rewards members who refer a new household.

Expected attendance: 100–150 patients. New patient acquisition: 20–30 households.
