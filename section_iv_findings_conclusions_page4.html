C. The SWASTIK Loyalty Framework

The six conclusions converge into a single strategic framework — Project Swastik — named for the ancient Sanskrit symbol (सवस्तिक) representing well-being, auspiciousness, and holistic health. The framework has three pillars, each addressing a specific loyalty driver identified in the research:

Pillar 1: Drishti (दृष्टि — "Vision")

Vision through transparency

Build trust through billing transparency and proactive communication. Before the patient picks up a prescription, they know the copay. Before insurance denies a medication, they get an alert. Before a refill is due, they get a reminder. Drishti is the foundation — without trust, no loyalty program works.

Key initiative: Pre-fill Copay Confirmation System + Automated Prior Auth Alerts

Pillar 2: Seva (सेवा — "Service")

Service through personalization

Build a household-based loyalty program that rewards health engagement, not just spending. Patients earn "wellness points" for filling prescriptions on time, attending free health screenings, referring family members, and purchasing OTC products. Points redeemable for OTC discounts, free screenings, and wellness products. Seva is the engine — it drives repeat behavior and increases lifetime value.

Key initiative: StarCare Wellness Rewards — a household account with family profiles, wellness points, and health-focused redemption options

Pillar 3: Milap (मिलाप — "Connection")

Connection through community roots

Build visible community presence through sponsorships, health screenings, and wellness workshops. StarCare becomes more than a pharmacy — it becomes a community health partner. Milap is the amplifier — it deepens emotional ties and turns patients into advocates who refer others.

Key initiative: StarCare Community Health Program — quarterly health screenings, school sponsorships, immunization clinics, and wellness workshops at Prosper community centers
