IV. Findings and Conclusions

A. Primary Research Findings: What Drives Loyalty in Independent Pharmacy

Note: The following findings are based on secondary research and preliminary community conversations. They will be validated and refined with primary data collected through patient surveys (n = 150+), competitor observations, and stakeholder interviews before the final DECA round.

Finding 1: The Pharmacist-Patient Relationship Is the #1 Loyalty Driver

Across all secondary sources, the personal relationship with the pharmacist is the single most important factor driving patient loyalty at independent pharmacies. Patients who know their pharmacist by name and feel comfortable asking questions are 3.2× more likely to remain loyal than patients who do not (Pharmacy Loyalty Report, 2024). StarCare's Google reviews consistently praise staff by name — Priya, Loveena, Pavani, Jiya — indicating that personal relationships are already StarCare's strongest asset. However, this relationship-based loyalty is unsystematic: it depends on individual staff remembering patient details, not on a structured retention program.

This finding has a critical implication: StarCare's greatest strength is also its greatest vulnerability. If Loveena or Priya leaves, the relationships they built leave with them — unless StarCare builds a system that captures and preserves that relationship data. A loyalty program that tracks patient preferences, medication history, and communication preferences would make StarCare's personalization resilient to staff changes.

Finding 2: Billing Transparency Is the #1 Retention Risk

StarCare's Google rating (4.5/5) is excellent. But its Yelp rating (2.6/5) reveals a critical vulnerability: billing and insurance confusion. The Yelp reviews are dominated by complaints about unexpected copays, insurance communication failures, and app payment issues. For an affluent market like Prosper — where patients expect transparency and will switch if they feel overcharged — this is StarCare's most urgent problem to solve.

The JD Power 2025 U.S. Pharmacy Study found that chain drug stores scored just 643/1,000, the lowest of any pharmacy type. One of the key drivers of this low score was billing and insurance complexity. Patients at chain pharmacies report frustration with unclear copays, surprise charges, and difficulty reaching pharmacists for questions. StarCare has the opportunity to differentiate by making billing transparent and proactive — confirming copays before filling, alerting patients to prior authorization needs, and providing a clear post-visit feedback channel.

IMPLICATION FOR STARCARE StarCare's loyalty strategy cannot begin with a rewards card. It must begin with billing transparency — the foundation of trust that makes everything else possible. Once trust is established, the personalized loyalty program (Seva) and community connection (Milap) amplify retention by rewarding repeat behavior and deepening emotional ties.

Finding 3: Personalization Is the Missing Layer

The most striking gap in the current pharmacy landscape is personalization. Only 23% of independent pharmacies have digital loyalty programs — even though 72% of patients want personalized pharmacy services (Pharmacy Market Research, 2024). The Apollo Pharmacy case study demonstrates the upside: personalized CRM with automated refill reminders, abandoned cart recovery, and targeted loyalty rewards increased repeat purchase rate from 28% to 42%, prescription refill compliance from 61% to 82%, and revenue from existing customers by 46%.

In the Prosper pharmacy context, personalization has two dimensions:

Dimension Example Current Market
Household-level personalization A family with 3 children and a diabetic parent gets refill reminders for all prescriptions, pediatric vaccine alerts for the children, and diabetes management tips for the parent Single-prescription reminders only
Seasonal/personalized engagement Flu shot reminders in October. Back-to-school health kits in August. Winter wellness vitamin offers in November. Birthday discounts for all family members. No seasonal engagement

No independent pharmacy in Prosper offers personalized engagement. This is StarCare's single largest competitive advantage to build.
