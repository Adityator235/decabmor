## Business Concept

**StarCare Pharmacy (StarcareRx)** is a family-owned independent community pharmacy at 210 S. Preston Rd., Suite B, Prosper, TX 75078. With a 4.5/5 Google rating (160+ reviews), free home delivery, compounding specialty services, and a veterinary medication program, StarCare serves Prosper's 45,600 residents — a market that grew 47.9% since 2020 with a $195,281 median household income.

**Project Swastik** is the three-pillar loyalty strategy:
- **Drishti (दृष्टि, "Vision")** — Billing transparency and trust through proactive copay confirmation and prior auth alerts
- **Seva (सेवा, "Service")** — Personalized wellness rewards that health engagement, not just spending
- **Milap (मिलाप, "Connection")** — Community roots through health screenings, school sponsorships, and wellness workshops
