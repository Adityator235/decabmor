# Key Terms Glossary — DECA BMOR

## A

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Anticipation Discount** | Additional discount for paying invoice prior to cash discount period |
| **Assortment** | The full range of product categories, styles, and sizes a store offers |
| **Assortment Breadth** | Number of different product lines a store carries |
| **Assortment Depth** | Number of SKUs within a single product line |

## B

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Blanket Order** | Long-term purchase agreement covering multiple deliveries over a period |
| **Breakeven Quantity** | Fixed Costs / (Selling Price - Variable Cost per Unit) — the sales volume where revenue equals costs |
| **Budget** | Detailed financial plan for merchandise purchases over a specific period |

## C

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Central Buying** | Completing all purchases through a central office |
| **Channel of Distribution** | Path goods take from producer to consumer |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** | Total revenue a business can expect from a single customer account |
| **Cumulative Discount** | Discount that applies to orders placed over a period of time |

## D

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Days Sales of Inventory (DSI)** | (Average Inventory / COGS) × 365 — how many days to sell inventory |
| **Direct Buyer** | Orders directly from manufacturers |
| **Drop Shipment** | Vendor ships directly to customer, bypassing retailer's warehouse |

## E

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Economic Order Quantity (EOQ)** | Optimal order size that minimizes total inventory costs |
| **EDI (Electronic Data Interchange)** | Computer-to-computer communication of business documents between companies |
| **Floor Ready Merchandise** | Packaging, folding, hanging, ticketing so merchandise is ready for the selling floor |

## F

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **FIFO** | First-In, First-Out — oldest stock sold first |
| **Forward Stock** | Merchandise displayed on the selling floor |

## G

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Gross Margin %** | (Net Sales - COGS) / Net Sales × 100 — profit percentage based on retail price |
| **GMROI** | Gross Margin Return on Investment — Gross Margin $ / Average Inventory at Cost |

## I

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Initial Markup** | Markup added to cost to establish the first retail price |
| **Inventory Turnover** | COGS / Average Inventory — how many times inventory sells in a period |

## K

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Keystoning** | Doubling an item's cost to establish a retail price (50% gross margin) |

## L

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Lead Time** | Elapsed time between submitting a purchase order and receiving goods |
| **Loss Leader** | Product sold below cost to attract traffic for higher-margin items |

## M

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Maintained Markup** | Difference between cost and actual selling price after all reductions |
| **Markdown** | Downward adjustment in retail price |
| **Merchandise Mix** | Combination of product lines and brands a retailer offers |
| **Micromerchandising** | Tailoring a store's product mix to local tastes and demographics |
| **Markup %** | (Selling Price - Cost) / Cost × 100 — profit percentage based on cost |
| **Marketing-Information Management** | Gathering, analyzing, and distributing information about customers, competitors, and market trends |

## O

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Open-to-Buy (OTB)** | Amount of merchandise a buyer needs to order to support planned sales while meeting inventory goals |
| **On-Order** | Merchandise ordered from vendors but not yet received |

## P

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Penetration Pricing** | Setting a low initial price to gain market share quickly |
| **Planogram** | Visual diagram showing product placement on shelves to maximize sales |
| **Price Elasticity of Demand (PED)** | % Change in Quantity / % Change in Price — measures price sensitivity |
| **Price Skimming** | Launching at a high price, then lowering over time |
| **Product Depth** | Number of SKUs within a single product line |
| **Product Breadth** | Number of product lines carried |
| **Profit** | Net Sales - Cost of Goods Sold - Operating Expenses |
| **Promotion** | Activities that communicate the merits of a product and stimulate selling |

## R

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Reorder Point** | (Average Daily Usage × Lead Time) + Safety Stock — when to place a new order |
| **Retention Rate** | Percentage of customers who continue to buy over a given period |

## S

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Safety Stock** | Reserve inventory held to absorb unexpected demand surges or shipping delays |
| **SKU (Stock-Keeping Unit)** | Unique tracking code for each distinct product variation |
| **Stock Turn** | How many times inventory is sold in a period; same as Inventory Turnover |
| **Stock-to-Sales Ratio** | BOM Stock $ / Monthly Sales $ — measures months of supply |
| **Strategic Planning** | Matching the firm's strengths to environmental forces while mitigating threats |

## U

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Unit Control Method** | Tracking merchandise in units — useful for fashion items |

## V

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Vendor Credit Memorandum (VCM)** | Document from vendor acknowledging credit for returns or overcharges |
| **Vendor Price Reduction (VPR)** | Post-arrival price reduction when demand was lower than expected |
| **Value-Based Pricing** | Setting price based on perceived benefit to customer rather than cost |

## Visual Merchandising

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Visual Merchandising** | Promoting sales through effective floor design, displays, and store layout |
| **Facings** | Number of product units visible on the shelf |
| **Eye-Level = Buy-Level** | Products at eye level sell best |
