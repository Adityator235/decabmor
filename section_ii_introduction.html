II. Introduction

A. Description of the Business

StarCare Pharmacy (operating as StarcareRx) is a family-owned independent specialty and compounding pharmacy located at 210 S. Preston Rd, Suite B, Prosper, TX 75078. The pharmacy serves Prosper and surrounding Collin County with a full spectrum of pharmacy services: prescription filling, medical counseling, custom drug compounding, immunizations, medication therapy management, durable medical equipment, veterinary medications, and free home delivery.

StarCare operates Monday–Friday 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, Saturday 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM, and Sunday closed. The pharmacy accepts all major insurance plans including BCBS, Medicare, Medicaid, Aetna, Cigna, and others. It offers online refill and transfer tools, walk-in service, and free home delivery within the Prosper service area.

StarCare's stated vision is "To be North Dallas's valued health partner." Its core values emphasize family-style personal attention, putting customers first, prioritizing health outcomes, maintaining transparency, honoring commitments, and striving for excellence. The pharmacy's guiding philosophy is: "Believe in Personal Care, Like Family... The Customer is at the Core of Everything we do!"

Despite this philosophy, StarCare has no formal loyalty program, no structured patient retention system, and no system for measuring patient satisfaction beyond online reviews. This entry examines how a structured loyalty strategy can transform StarCare's philosophy into measurable, repeatable business outcomes.

Prosper is a young, affluent, family-oriented community. With 32.7% of residents under 18 and 59.7% of households with children, the town's pharmacy demand is driven by families — not seniors, not singles, but parents. This is a critical distinction: the primary pharmacy decision-maker in Prosper is a parent managing prescriptions for children, themselves, and often aging parents. The loyalty strategy must reflect this household-centric model.
