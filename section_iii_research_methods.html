III. Research Methods

A. Research Design

This study employs a mixed-methods research design combining quantitative surveys, qualitative interviews, and structured competitor observations to answer the central research question: What drives customer loyalty and repeat engagement at independent community pharmacies in Prosper, Texas, and how can StarCare Pharmacy strengthen retention through a structured loyalty strategy?

The research follows a three-phase approach:

Phase Method Sample Objective
Phase 1 Patient Survey n = 150+ Quantify loyalty drivers, current pharmacy behavior, and loyalty program preferences
Phase 2 Competitor Observation 4 pharmacies × 2 visits each Document in-store experience, loyalty program presence, merchandising practices
Phase 3 Stakeholder Interviews n = 8–10 Deep-dive into patient motivations, pain points, and community expectations

B. Primary Research Plan

Phase 1: Patient Survey (n = 150+)

The patient survey will be distributed through three channels to ensure a representative sample:

Channel Target n Rationale
Online (Google Forms via Prosper community groups) 80–100 Reaches Prosper parent groups on Facebook, WhatsApp, and Nextdoor; captures both StarCare patients and patients of competing pharmacies
In-person (at StarCare Pharmacy) 30–50 Current StarCare patients provide direct insight into loyalty drivers and switching triggers from the perspective of patients who already chose StarCare
In-person (at competitor pharmacies) 20–30 Patients currently shopping at CVS, Walmart, H-E-B, and Costco provide comparative data on why patients switch and what drives loyalty to chains

The survey instrument will include 25–30 questions organized into five sections:

Section Question Types Key Variables
A. Pharmacy Habits Multiple choice, frequency Primary pharmacy, visit frequency, number of prescriptions filled, average spend
B. Loyalty Program Awareness Multiple choice, Likert scale Awareness of existing programs (ExtraBucks, price match), enrollment status, perceived value, redemption behavior
C. Loyalty Drivers Likert scale (1–5), ranking Price, convenience, pharmacist relationship, insurance transparency, community presence, delivery, personalization
D. Program Preferences Likert scale, multiple choice Preferred reward types (discounts, free screenings, referrals), preferred communication channel, tier structure preferences
E. Demographics Categorical Age, household size, children in household, income range, length of Prosper residence
SAMPLE SIZE JUSTIFICATION With an estimated population of 45,600 residents in Prosper and approximately 60% of households having at least one prescription, the addressable pharmacy market is approximately 18,000 households. A sample of n = 150 provides a margin of error of ±7.8% at 95% confidence level. This is sufficient for descriptive statistics and segment-level analysis.
