B. Description of the Primary Products or Services

Based on the StarCareRx website and Google Maps listing, here's what StarCare offers and why each service matters for customer loyalty:

ServiceDescriptionWhy It Matters for Loyalty
Prescription FillingNew and refill prescriptionsPrimary traffic driver — this is what brings people in every day. Chronic medication patients generate about $3,200 in annual value.
Custom CompoundingCustom medication formulationsSomething the chains can't do — real differentiator. Patients who use compounding report very high satisfaction.
ImmunizationsCOVID-19, flu, and other vaccines — walk-in acceptedHigh-frequency touchpoint — gets people in the door regularly, especially during flu season.
Medication Therapy ManagementComprehensive medication reviewsBuilds deeper relationships with patients. Shows patients you care about their health, not just their wallet.
Durable Medical EquipmentMedical supplies and equipmentSticky revenue — patients stay for months or years. Once they start using StarCare for supplies, they don't switch.
Veterinary MedicationsAnimal health prescriptionsUnique differentiator — very few chains do this well. Creates loyalty among pet owners.
Free Home DeliveryComplimentary doorstep shippingRetention tool — builds habit and dependence. Patients who use delivery are less likely to switch.
Online Refill Portal24/7 digital refill submissionDigital engagement channel. Makes it easy for patients to stay.
Online Transfer PortalDigital prescription transferCustomer acquisition — makes it easy to switch to StarCare from another pharmacy.
Wellness ProgramsHealth consultations and screeningsCommunity connection pillar. Positions StarCare as a health partner, not just a prescription filler.
Greeting Cards & GiftsNon-pharmaceutical merchandiseOTC revenue driver — high-margin impulse purchases. Texas-themed items reinforce local identity.
