# DECA Buying and Merchandising Operations — Competition Overview

## Event Format

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Event Type** | Prepared Entry + Oral Presentation |
| **Team Size** | 1-3 members (all must present) |
| **Written Entry** | Maximum 20 numbered pages (excluding title page and table of contents) |
| **Presentation** | 15 minutes max, including judge's questions |
| **Exam** | 100-item multiple-choice (Marketing Cluster Exam) |
| **Total Score** | Written Entry (100) + Presentation (100) = 200 points |

## 2026-2027 Competition Topic

**"Rebuilding Customer Loyalty and Spending"**

Collaborate with a local business or organization to:
1. **Research** what drives customer loyalty and repeat engagement
2. **Develop** a strategic plan to strengthen customer retention through:
   - Loyalty programs
   - Personalized experiences
   - Relationship-building initiatives

**Sample Company**: Combat Flip Flops (veteran-owned business)

## Acceptable Business Types

Buying and Merchandising: Any retail or wholesale business that provides consumer goods:
- Specialty stores
- Department stores
- Shopping malls
- Grocery stores
- Convenience stores
- Pharmacies
- Discount stores
- Farmers markets
- Car dealerships

## Entry Requirements

### Written Entry (100 points)
- Maximum **20 numbered pages** including appendix, excluding title page and TOC
- First page numbered "1" — page numbers continue through bibliography and appendix
- Must follow the **exact written entry outline** (see below)
- Must pass the **Penalty Point Checklist**
- Must include signed **Statement of Assurances and Academic Integrity Form** (not counted in pages)
- Bibliography is **required**
- Appendix is **optional**

### Presentation (100 points)
- **Role-play**: Participants = hired consultants; Judge = business owner/manager
- Presentation begins immediately after introductions by adult assistant
- Each participant **must take part**
- Explain the proposed strategic plan at the start — let you complete without interruption unless judge asks a question
- Hand-carried visual aids only (no wheeled carts, moving straps, generators)
- **No electrical power or internet connection** — you furnish your own materials
- Setup time is **included in the 15 minutes**
- Materials like flyers, brochures, pamphlets, and business cards may be handed to or left with judges
- Items of monetary value may be handed to but **not left** with judges
- No food or drinks allowed
- Sound may be used at conversational volume

### Written Entry Structure (Required)

| Section | Pages | Point Value |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| **I. Executive Summary** | 1-3 pages | 10 |
| **II. Introduction** | | 8 |
| A. Description of the business (purpose, products/services) | | |
| B. Target market (demographics and psychographics) | | |
| C. Current business strategy relating to customer loyalty | | |
| **III. Research Methods Used in the Study** | | 16 |
| A. Description and rationale of research methodologies | | |
| B. Process used to conduct the selected research methods | | |
| **IV. Findings and Conclusions of the Study** | | 16 |
| A. Findings of the research study | | |
| B. Conclusions based on the findings | | |
| **V. Proposed Strategic Plan** | | **36** |
| A. Objectives and rationale of the proposed strategic plan | | |
| B. Proposed activities and timelines | | |
| C. Proposed metrics/KPIs to measure plan effectiveness | | |
| **VI. Proposed Budget** | | 8 |
| **VII. Bibliography** | (required, not counted) | — |
| **VIII. Appendix** | (optional, numbered) | — |

**Key**: The Strategic Plan section is worth **36 out of 100 points** — it is the single most important section. Spend the most time here.

## Preparation Timeline

| Timeline | Action |
|----------|--------|
| **8-12 weeks out** | Select business, interview management, develop survey tools, begin secondary research |
| **4-7 weeks out** | Distribute surveys (50-100+ respondents), analyze data, draft 20-page report, calculate budgets/ROI |
| **1-3 weeks out** | Finalize formatting, get signatures on forms, design visual aids, conduct 3+ mock presentations |

## Key Reminders

- **Primary research is mandatory** — surveys, interviews, observations from the partner business
- **Focus on buying & merchandising** — not general marketing
- **Include a detailed budget and implementation timeline**
- **Write the executive summary last** — make it dynamic with key financial data
- **Practice with mock judges** — anticipate questions on ROI, risks, and budget justification
- **Professional delivery matters** — dress professionally, make eye contact, vary tone
- **You are hired consultants; the judge is the business owner** — stay in character
- **Setup time counts toward your 15 minutes** — practice quick setup
- **No electrical power or internet** — prepare for no-projection presentation

## Common Penalty Triggers

- Exceeding the 20-page limit (excluding title page, TOC)
- Not including the Statement of Assurances
- Unsigned academic integrity form
- Missing required sections
- Generic proposal not specific to the business
- No primary research included
- Not focused on buying/merchandising (general marketing instead)
