V. Strategic Plan: Summary & Year 2 Outlook

Strategic Plan Summary

Project Swastik translates six research findings into five strategic initiatives, organized under three pillars. Here is the complete plan at a glance:

👁️ Drishti (Months 1–3)

  • Pre-fill copay confirmation workflow
  • Automated prior auth alerts
  • Wellness Rewards pilot (50 households)
  • Baseline metrics established

🤝 Seva (Months 4–8)

  • Full Wellness Rewards launch
  • Household accounts operational
  • Personalization engine active
  • Wellness workshops begin

🌱 Milap (Months 9–12)

  • Community health program Q1 event
  • School sponsorships secured
  • Chamber of Commerce membership
  • Year-end review & Year 2 plan

📊 Measured Outcomes

  • Return rate: 42% → 78%
  • Rx/household: 8 → 12/year
  • Google rating: 4.5 → 4.7
  • ROI: 491% (base case)

Year 2 Outlook: Scaling What Works

If Project Swastik achieves its Year 1 targets, Year 2 focuses on scaling and deepening the loyalty engine. The following initiatives build on Year 1 foundations:

Year 2 Expansion Roadmap

Q1 Y2

Digital Wellness Card: Replace paper card with a simple mobile-friendly digital card (QR code stored in phone bookmark). Enable online point tracking and personalized offers.

Q2 Y2

Advanced Personalization: Implement segment-specific OTC product recommendations based on household profile (e.g., family with infant gets diaper rash cream reminders; diabetic household gets foot care products).

Q3 Y2

Community Health Ambassador Program: Train loyal Star members (Star-tier patients) to serve as community health ambassadors — hosting wellness tables at Prosper ISD events, sharing their health journey stories, and referring new households.

Q4 Y2

Year 2 Scorecard &> Expansion Analysis: Comprehensive program review. Evaluate expansion to second location (Frisco) based on Year 1 performance, brand recognition, and patient demand.

Strategic Conclusion

Project Swastik is not a loyalty program. It is a loyalty system — a complete framework that transforms StarCare Pharmacy from a transactional prescription dispenser into a community health partner. It works because it is built on six evidence-based findings, not assumptions. It is designed for StarCare's actual patients, not an abstract market. And it is measurable at every step: every initiative has a KPI, every KPI has a target, and every target has a baseline.

The numbers are clear: $14,000 investment, $68,720 net Year 1 gain, 491% ROI. But the true value of Project Swastik extends beyond revenue. It builds a pharmacy that is known, trusted, and rooted in the community it serves. In a market dominated by chains, that is the unfair advantage that no scale can replicate.
