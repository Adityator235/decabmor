# The Game Table — 3-Minute Video Script

**Team:** [Your Team Name]
**School:** [High School Name]
**Event:** DECA Buying and Merchandising Operations
**Topic:** Rebuilding Customer Loyalty and Spending
**Time:** ~3:00

---

## Slide 1: Title (0:00–0:10)

Hi, we're [names] from [school name], and this is The Game Table — a board game cafe and retail store in Frisco, Texas. Our presentation is about rebuilding customer loyalty and spending.

## Slide 2: The Problem (0:10–0:35)

Here's the problem. More and more people, especially teenagers and young adults, are spending way too much time on their phones. A study found that 53 percent of consumers are trying to use their phones less. That number went up 20 points in just two years. This is a huge problem for regular stores because customers aren't coming out as much. Traditional retail is losing people because nothing gives them a reason to leave their houses and hang out in person.

## Slide 3: Our Solution (0:35–1:00)

So here's our solution. The Game Table is a board game cafe where customers pay a cover charge and play any board game from our library of 300 to 800 games while they order food and drinks. It's what businesses call a "third place" — not home, not school. A real place to hang out, meet friends, and actually talk to each other. We're located just 15 minutes from UT Dallas, which has over 38,000 students. That's our built-in customer base.

## Slide 4: Market Opportunity (1:00–1:20)

The board game industry is worth $1.74 billion right now and is expected to reach $3.84 billion by 2034. That's a 9.2 percent growth rate every year. This is not a trend. This is a real, growing industry backed by government data and market research. Our main customers are Gen Z students who visit often and spend $18 to $28 per visit, and Millennials who spend $35 to $48 per visit but come a little less often.

## Slide 5: Competitive Gap (1:20–1:40)

Now here's where it gets interesting. We looked at our competitors in Frisco. Nerdvana opens at 4 PM and takes no reservations. Texas Tabletop only opens from 6 to 10 PM and is members-only on Sundays. Farm+Feed is more of a gaming restaurant than a community space. No competitor in Frisco serves families and students during the day. So we're going to open at 10 AM and own the entire daytime market.

## Slide 6: Key Findings (1:40–2:00)

Our research found four things. First, screen fatigue is real — 53 percent of people want less screen time. Second, food and drinks make the most money — 60 to 70 percent of revenue comes from the cafe side. Third, loyalty comes from community, not discounts. And fourth, the pre-owned game market is growing 5.5 percent per year.

## Slide 7: Turn the P.A.G.E. (2:00–2:30)

So our strategic plan is called "Turn the P.A.G.E." P is for Pre-Owned — a marketplace where customers sell their old games back to us. A is for App-Based — a mobile app with push notifications, customer voting on new games, and loyalty points. G is for Giving — each month we support a different cause like a food bank or mental health organization. And E is for Employee Engagement — better pay, flexible scheduling, and weekly service bonuses to keep our staff happy.

## Slide 8–11: The Four Pillars (2:30–2:45)

We're starting with the pre-owned program in the first quarter, launching the app by the third quarter, supporting 12 causes a year, and giving our employees real incentives to provide great customer service.

## Slide 12–14: Financials and KPIs (2:45–2:55)

Our campaign budget is $14,000. The total startup cost for The Game Table is between $201,000 and $460,000. Our return on investment is 420 percent. That means for every dollar we spend on the campaign, we make $4.20 back. We expect to break even in the second quarter of 2027.

## Slide 15–16: Closing (2:55–3:00)

Zero competitors serve daytime customers in Frisco. The board game industry is growing. And 53 percent of people want to put their phones down. The Game Table is where the community plays together. Thank you.

## Delivery Tips

1. **Speak slowly.** You'll want to speak faster than normal. Don't. Pause between slides.
2. **Practice with a timer.** 3 minutes goes by fast. If you're over 3:10, trim the financials section.
3. **Record in a quiet room.** Your phone's voice memo app + camera works fine. Face the camera, not the screen.
4. **Show the slides.** You can record your screen playing through the presentation.html file while you talk.
5. **Eye contact.** Look at the camera lens, not yourself on screen.
6. **Smile.** Seriously. It changes your voice and makes you sound confident.

## Equipment Needed

- Any smartphone or webcam
- Free screen recording: OBS Studio (obsproject.com) or your phone's built-in screen recorder
- Free video editor: DaVinci Resolve (davinciresolve.com) or even iMovie
- Optional: $15 USB microphone from Amazon for better audio
