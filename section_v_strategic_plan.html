V. Strategic Plan: Project Swastik

A. Strategic Overview

Project Swastik is a 12-month, three-pillar loyalty strategy designed to increase StarCare Pharmacy's patient return rate from the industry average of 42% to 78%, while building a sustainable competitive moat that chains cannot replicate. The plan is organized into three phases, each building on the previous:

Phase 1: Months 1–3 — Foundation

Drishti (दृष्टि): Build Trust Through Transparency

Implement billing transparency systems that eliminate the #1 retention risk: insurance confusion. Launch the StarCare Wellness Rewards pilot program. Establish baseline metrics.

Phase 2: Months 4–8 — Engagement

Seva (सेवा): Drive Repeat Behavior Through Personalized Wellness

Launch full Wellness Rewards program with household accounts, wellness points, and health-focused redemption. Implement pharmacist-led wellness check-ins. Begin seasonal engagement campaigns.

Phase 3: Months 9–12 — Community

Milap (मिलाप): Amplify Loyalty Through Community Roots

Launch community health program with quarterly screenings, school sponsorships, and wellness workshops. Measure referral-driven growth. Prepare Year 2 expansion plan.

C. Pillar 1: Drishti — Billing Transparency & Trust (Months 1–3)

Problem: StarCare's Yelp rating (2.6/5) vs. Google rating (4.5/5) reveals a critical trust gap. Yelp reviews are dominated by billing and insurance complaints — unexpected copays, poor communication, and app payment issues. Before any loyalty program can work, StarCare must eliminate this trust deficit.

Initiative 1.1: Pre-Fill Copay Confirmation System

Implement a simple system: before filling each prescription, the pharmacist calls the patient to confirm the estimated copay. This is not a technical build — it is a process change that takes 30 seconds per prescription and eliminates surprise charges. For a pharmacy filling ~200 prescriptions per week, this adds approximately 100 minutes per week (25 minutes per day) to pharmacist workflow — a negligible investment for the trust it builds.

Expected outcome: 80% reduction in billing-related complaints. Google rating increases from 4.5 to 4.7. Yelp rating converges toward 4.0+ as resolved patients update reviews.

Initiative 1.2: Automated Prior Authorization Alerts

Implement a workflow where prior authorization (PA) requests are identified before the prescription is filled, and the patient is proactively notified. Currently, PAs are often discovered after the fact, leading to delays and frustration. A simple PA checklist — reviewed by the pharmacist during prescription processing — ensures patients are notified of potential delays within 24 hours of prescription submission.

Expected outcome: 50% reduction in PA-related complaints. Patient satisfaction with prescription fill time increases from "fair" to "excellent" (measured via post-visit survey).

WHY THIS COMES FIRST Trust is the foundation of loyalty. No patient will join a rewards program at a pharmacy they do not trust to charge them fairly. Drishti is not a loyalty program — it is the prerequisite for one.
