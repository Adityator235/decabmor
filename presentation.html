Research Findings

What We Found

1. Screen Fatigue Is Real

53% of consumers want less screen time. Up 20 points in two years. People want to hang out in person.

3. Loyalty = Community

Customers don't care about points. They care about feeling like it's their place. Staff who know their names.

70% of students prefer used games. Second-hand market growing 5.5% per year. $45B opportunity.

P — Pre-Owned Marketplace

Customers Sell Games Back to Us

How It Works

Customers bring their old games to The Game Table. We buy them at fair prices using ISBN numbers, then resell them in our store at a 40–55% markup.

Why It Works

70% of students prefer used games. The second-hand market is growing 5.5% per year. Customers get money back, we get inventory, everyone wins.
