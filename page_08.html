III. Research Methods Used in the Study

A. Description and Rationale of Research Methodologies

This entry uses a mixed-methods approach, combining secondary research (industry reports, the JD Power pharmacy study, Census data, Google Maps analysis) with planned primary research (patient surveys, competitor observations, and stakeholder interviews). The secondary research gives me a baseline understanding of the pharmacy market and loyalty trends. The primary research will fill in the gaps that existing data can't address — specifically, what StarCare's actual patients think and why they come back (or don't).

I chose a mixed-methods approach because no single source of data can answer the full question. Secondary data tells me what's happening at the market level — return rates, satisfaction scores, demographic trends. But it doesn't tell me what StarCare's specific patients think. Primary data is needed to answer the question: why do patients choose StarCare, and why do they leave?
