C. Current Operations and Competitive Landscape

Prosper's pharmacy market is highly competitive, dominated by major chains. StarCare competes against CVS (multiple locations), Walmart Pharmacy, H-E-B Pharmacy, and Costco Pharmacy — all of which have scale, digital infrastructure, and loyalty programs that StarCare lacks. However, the JD Power 2025 U.S. Pharmacy Study reveals that chain drug stores scored just 643/1,000 — the lowest of any pharmacy type — while chain drug store customers are significantly more likely to switch pharmacies in the next 12 months than any other segment. This creates a unique opportunity for StarCare to capture and retain displaced patients.

Market Gap Analysis

The competitive landscape reveals three clear gaps that StarCare can exploit:

Gap 1: Personalized care without a loyalty system. StarCare already delivers personalized care — staff know patients by name, reviews praise individual pharmacists. But this personalization is unsystematic: it depends on individual staff remembering details, not on a structured retention program. A loyalty program would formalize what StarCare already does well, making it repeatable regardless of staff changes.

Gap 2: Billing transparency. StarCare's Yelp rating (2.6/5) is significantly lower than its Google rating (4.5/5), and the Yelp reviews are dominated by billing and insurance complaints. This is a fixable problem: a pre-fill copay confirmation system, automated prior authorization alerts, and a post-visit satisfaction survey would eliminate the #1 driver of patient attrition.

Gap 3: Community integration. No independent pharmacy in Prosper has a structured community engagement strategy. StarCare's "health partner" vision is aspirational — but it requires visible community presence: health screenings, school sponsorships, immunization clinics. Chains have loyalty programs; StarCare can have community roots.

Threats

  • Amazon Pharmacy (58% brand recognition)
  • PBM practices disadvantage independents
  • Staff turnover at small pharmacy
  • Insurance network changes
