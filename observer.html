StarCare Pharmacy — DECA BMOR 2026-27

📖 Visit Guide & Busy Time Research

How to Measure Wait Time

Add each customer when they join the line. Tap "Served" when they get their meds. The app averages all wait times. Do this across multiple visits at different times to capture busy vs slow patterns.

CVS — Typical Traffic

  • Busy: 12-1 PM, 4-6 PM Sat
  • Medium: 10-11 AM, 2-3 PM
  • Slow: 8-9 AM weekdays, 1-2 PM weekdays

CVS has drive-thru at most locations. Expect longer waits during lunch rush and Saturday morning. Peak Saturday is usually 10 AM - 12 PM.

H-E-B Pharmacy — Typical Traffic

  • Busy: 5-7 PM weekdays, 9-11 AM Sat
  • Medium: 3-4 PM, 12-1 PM
  • Slow: 8-9 AM weekdays, 2-4 PM weekdays

H-E-B pharmacy is inside the grocery store. Traffic spikes with grocery rush. Saturday morning is the busiest — people do grocery + pharmacy together. Weekday evenings see after-work pickups.

Prosper Pharmacy — Typical Traffic

  • Busy: 3-5 PM weekdays, 10-12 PM Sat
  • Medium: 11 AM-12 PM, 2-3 PM
  • Slow: 8-9 AM, 1-2 PM

Smaller independent — less predictable but generally slower than chains. Morning rush 3-5 PM when people pick up after work. Saturday mornings busy with local regulars.

Data Points to Collect at Every Visit

  • Wait time (queue tracking — add each customer, mark served)
  • Customers in store at time of visit
  • Staff on duty (pharmacists + techs at counter)
  • Customer-to-staff ratio
  • Drive-thru available? If yes, cars in line
  • Staff interaction quality (1-5)
  • OTC/wellness display near counter (Low/Med/High)
  • Loyalty program visible? (Yes/No + details)
  • Store atmosphere & cleanliness (1-5)
  • Digital engagement (app, WiFi, screens)
  • Prescription drop-off vs pickup separate?
  • Specialty pharmacy on-site?
  • Health screenings available?
  • Staff uses customer names? (Yes/No)
  • Staff counsels on medications? (Yes/No)
  • Overall impression (1-5)
