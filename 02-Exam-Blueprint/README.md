# DECA BMOR — Exam Blueprint & Study Priorities

## Exam Overview

- **Format**: 100-item, multiple-choice test
- **Exam Type**: Marketing Cluster Exam (shared with Apparel & Accessories Marketing, Food Marketing, Marketing Management)
- **Scoring**: Exam scores are averaged between team members; written entry + presentation weighted at 2x exam scores

## Study Priority Strategy

### 🔴 HIGHEST PRIORITY (40% of exam)
**Marketing-Information Management** + **Product/Service Management**
- These two areas alone account for ~25% of questions at District, ~27% at Association, and ~31% at ICDC
- Focus on: merchandise planning, assortment, inventory, pricing, product lifecycle
- **This is where you'll spend most of your study time**

### 🟠 HIGH PRIORITY (45% of exam)
**Promotion** + **Emotional Intelligence** + **Selling**
- Critical for both the exam AND the oral presentation
- Focus on: customer psychology, selling techniques, promotional strategies
- These directly translate to presentation skills

### 🟡 MEDIUM PRIORITY (15% of exam)
All remaining areas with 2-6 questions each
- Focus on: financial analysis, economics, channel management, operations
- These areas contain the retail math formulas — know them cold

### 🟢 LOW PRIORITY (5% of exam)
Areas with 0-1 questions
- Light review only; don't spend significant time here

## Exam-Level Differences

| Level | Total Questions from Top 2 Areas | Focus |
|-------|----------------------------------|-------|
| **District** | ~22 (22%) | Foundational concepts, straightforward calculations |
| **Association** | ~27 (27%) | Applied scenarios, multi-step calculations |
| **ICDC** | ~31 (31%) | Complex analysis, strategic thinking, data interpretation |

## What to Study by Priority Area

### Marketing-Information Management
- Market research methods
- Data analysis and interpretation
- Customer information systems
- Marketing ethics and legal issues
- Technology in marketing

### Product/Service Management
- Merchandise planning and buying
- Assortment strategy (breadth vs. depth)
- Product lifecycle
- Private label vs. national brands
- Store brands and exclusives

### Promotion
- Advertising, sales promotion, public relations
- Promotional mix strategies
- Visual merchandising and displays
- Seasonal and event promotions

### Emotional Intelligence
- Self-awareness in customer interactions
- Empathy in service recovery
- Managing stress in retail environments
- Building relationships with customers and team

### Selling
- Selling process and techniques
- Handling objections
- Closing techniques
- Personal selling ethics
