I. Executive Summary

StarCare Pharmacy (StarcareRx) is an independent community pharmacy at 210 S. Preston Rd, Suite B, Prosper, TX 75078, serving Prosper and surrounding Collin County. Prosper is one of the fastest-growing and most affluent cities in the United States — a town of 45,600+ residents with a median household income of $195,281, the highest in Texas. StarCare is a family-owned specialty and compounding pharmacy with a 4.5-star Google rating from 160+ reviews, free home delivery, and a philosophy of "personal care, like family." Yet despite strong reviews, StarCare faces a critical challenge: no formal loyalty program exists, billing and insurance confusion drives away customers who cannot afford to lose a single patient household, and major chains (CVS, Walmart, H-E-B, Costco) are actively poaching StarCare's customer base.

THE OPPORTUNITY Chain drug stores scored just 643 out of 1,000 in the JD Power 2025 U.S. Pharmacy Study — the lowest of any pharmacy type, 72 points below supermarkets and 54 points below mail order. Chain drug store customers are significantly more likely to switch pharmacies in the next 12 months than any other segment. This is not a threat to StarCare — it is an opening. But StarCare must be ready to capture and retain those customers. Right now, it has no loyalty program, no structured retention strategy, and no system for turning one-time visitors into lifetime patients.

The Problem

Independent pharmacies lose 3–5% of their customer base annually. The average return rate at independent pharmacies is just 42% — meaning nearly 6 out of 10 patients who fill a prescription at StarCare once will not return. Meanwhile, 58% of customers will switch pharmacies if they find better convenience (Pharmacy Market Research, 2024), and only 23% of independent pharmacies have digital loyalty programs — even though 72% of customers want personalized pharmacy services.

StarCare's specific challenges are threefold: (1) No loyalty infrastructure — StarCare has no formal loyalty program, no prescription refill reminder system, and no structured way to track patient retention. (2) Billing and insurance transparency — despite a 4.5-star Google rating, StarCare's Yelp rating is 2.6/5, driven by complaints about insurance billing errors, unexpected copays, and app payment failures. (3) Chain pharmacy competition — CVS operates multiple locations in Prosper, Walmart and H-E-B have pharmacies in town, and Costco Pharmacy (765/1,000 JD Power score) is the second-highest-rated pharmacy type. Prosper's affluent, young families are highly price-sensitive despite high incomes — they will switch for convenience but will stay for trust.

The Solution: Project Swastik

Project Swastik (स्वस्तिक — "conducive to well-being" in Sanskrit) is a three-pillar loyalty strategy designed specifically for independent pharmacy:

Pillar 1 — Drishti (दृष्टि, "Vision"): Transparent Care. A billing transparency initiative that eliminates insurance confusion before it happens. Every patient receives a clear, written explanation of their copay before filling — not after. Automated prior authorization alerts 3–5 days before the due date. A post-visit satisfaction survey (via WhatsApp or SMS) catches billing issues within 24 hours. This addresses StarCare's #1 complaint driver: billing and insurance confusion.

Pillar 2 — Seva (सेवा, "Service"): Personalized Wellness. A tiered loyalty program that rewards prescription refill consistency, OTC/wellness purchases, and referral behavior. Prescription refills earn stamps (hybrid stamp/points model). OTC and wellness purchases earn points redeemable for health screenings, vitamin bottles, or product discounts. Tiered benefits: Seed → Herb → Wellness → Swastik. Modeled on Apollo Pharmacy's CRM system, which achieved a 28%→42% repeat purchase rate and 61%→82% prescription refill compliance.

Pillar 3 — Milap (मिलाप, "Connection"): Community Connection. Community health events, local sponsorships, and relationship-building that turns StarCare from a pharmacy into a health partner. Free blood pressure screenings at local schools. Immunization clinics at Prosper ISD community events. Sponsorship of Prosper High School athletics and PISD academic programs. Personalized follow-up calls when a patient starts a new medication. This leverages StarCare's greatest competitive advantage over chains: personal relationships.

WHY THIS MODEL WORKS Prescription fills drive foot traffic and create recurring revenue (chronic medication patients generate ~$3,200 in annual value). OTC and wellness products generate the highest margins (45–60% gross margin on vitamins, personal care, and OTC products). Durable medical equipment and veterinary medications create differentiation that chains cannot match — no chain pharmacy has a veterinary medication program. The loyalty program increases prescription refill consistency (+34%), boosts OTC/wellness spend (+28–37%), and deepens community ties that make switching to a chain feel impersonal.

Projected Outcomes

Project Swastik targets a 42%→78% increase in patient return rate, a 68% prescription refill compliance rate, and a 35% increase in OTC/wellness revenue per patient within six months of launch. Total projected Year 1 revenue: $1.8 million, with break-even achieved in 3.2 months.
