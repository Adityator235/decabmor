VIII. Appendix

A. Customer Survey Instrument (Planned)

The customer survey will include 25–30 questions organized into five sections. A copy of the final survey instrument will be included here following primary research completion.

Section A — Shopping Habits (5 questions):

  1. How often do you shop at a South Asian grocery store? (Daily / Weekly / Biweekly / Monthly / Rarely)
  2. Which South Asian grocery stores do you shop at most often? (Open response + multiple choice)
  3. How much do you typically spend per visit? ($50–$100 / $100–$200 / $200–$300 / $300+)
  4. How many South Asian grocery stores do you shop at regularly? (1 / 2 / 3 / 4+)
  5. What is the primary factor that determines which store you shop? (Price / Selection / Freshness / Location / Service)

Section B — Loyalty Program Awareness (5 questions):

  1. Are you aware of any loyalty programs at South Asian grocery stores in your area? (Yes / No)
  2. Which loyalty programs are you enrolled in? (Patel Brothers / India Bazaar / None / Other)
  3. How often do you use your loyalty benefits? (Always / Often / Sometimes / Rarely / Never)
  4. On a scale of 1–5, how valuable are loyalty programs to you? (Likert scale)
  5. What would make you use a loyalty program more often? (Open response)

Section C — Loyalty Drivers (8 questions):

  1. Rate the importance of each factor in determining your store loyalty (1–5 Likert): product availability, price fairness, customer service, cultural relevance, loyalty program, location, store cleanliness, fresh produce quality
  2. Rank your top 3 factors (ranking question)
  3. Would you switch stores for a personalized loyalty program? (Definitely / Probably / Unlikely / Definitely not)
  4. What type of reward would motivate you most? (Percentage discount / Free product / Bonus points / Exclusive access)
  5. How important is it that a store celebrates cultural festivals? (1–5 Likert)
  6. Would you visit a store more often if they hosted cultural events? (Yes / No / Maybe)
  7. What cultural events would you like to see celebrated? (Open response: Diwali, Eid, Navratri, Holi, Onam, Vishu, Pohela Boishakh, other)
  8. How likely are you to recommend a store to a friend or family member? (0–10 NPS scale)

B. Competitor Observation Protocol

Each competitor store will be observed for a 90-minute window during two time periods (weekday afternoon, weekend morning). Observers will record: store traffic (customers per 15 minutes), average basket size (visual estimate), loyalty program visibility (signage count), seasonal merchandising (display count), and staff-customer interactions (greeting count, issue resolution count).

C. Interview Protocol

Each interview will follow a semi-structured protocol with 8–10 open-ended questions, including: "What makes you loyal to a particular grocery store?" "What would make you switch to a new store?" "How do you learn about new stores or products?" "What role do cultural events play in your shopping?" "What would a perfect loyalty program look like to you?"
