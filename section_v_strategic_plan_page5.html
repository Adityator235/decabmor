Risk Mitigation

Risk Likelihood Mitigation
Staff resistance to new workflow Medium Frame as opportunity, not burden; tie to wellness rewards tier achievements; recognize top performers
Low patient enrollment in Wellness Rewards Low Launch with pilot program (50 households); use in-store signage and pharmacist recommendation to drive sign-ups
Competitor retaliation (CVS/Walmart match program) Medium Chains cannot replicate personalization; double down on community roots and pharmacist relationships
Budget overrun on community events Low Phase community spending to Phase 3; secure school sponsorship deals before Q1 event; use in-store space to minimize venue costs
MEASUREMENT PHILOSOPHY Every initiative in Project Swastik has a specific, measurable outcome. We track: patient return rate (primary KPI), prescriptions per household (secondary KPI), patient referrals (tertiary KPI), Google rating (trust proxy), and Wellness Rewards enrollment (program health). These metrics are reviewed monthly and reported to StarCare ownership.
