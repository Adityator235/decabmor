IX. Appendix

A. Patient Survey Instrument (Draft)

The following draft survey instrument will be used for primary data collection. Questions will be finalized after pilot testing with 10–15 patients.

Section A: Pharmacy Habits

A1. Which pharmacy(ies) do you currently use for prescriptions? (Select all that apply)
A2. How many prescriptions does your household fill per year? (0–3 / 4–7 / 8–12 / 13+)
A3. What is the primary reason you chose your current pharmacy? (Price / Location / Pharmacist relationship / Insurance / Other)
A4. How often do you purchase OTC products when you pick up a prescription? (Always / Usually / Sometimes / Rarely)
A5. Approximately how much do you spend at your pharmacy per month? (Under $25 / $25–$50 / $51–$100 / Over $100)

Section B: Loyalty Program Awareness

B1. Are you currently enrolled in any pharmacy loyalty programs? (CVS ExtraBucks / Walmart Price Match / Costco Price Match / None)
B2. How often do you use your loyalty rewards? (Every time / Sometimes / Never)
B3. Has a loyalty program ever convinced you to switch pharmacies? (Yes / No / Not sure)
B4. On a scale of 1–5, how important are loyalty rewards when choosing a pharmacy? (1 = not important, 5 = very important)

Section C: Loyalty Drivers (Rank by Importance)

C1. Please rank the following factors by importance when choosing a pharmacy: (1 = most important)
___ Low copay price   ___ Pharmacist relationship  ___ Convenience/location
___ Transparent billing  ___ Free delivery  ___ Personalized health advice
___ Loyalty rewards  ___ Community presence  ___ Online/app tools

Section D: Program Preferences

D1. Which of the following Wellness Rewards benefits would you value most? (Select top 3)
___ Free health screenings  ___ Points for OTC discounts  ___ Refill reminders
___ Family-wide account  ___ Referral bonuses  ___ Wellness workshop access
D2. Would you enroll in a pharmacy loyalty program that rewards health engagement (not just spending)? (Definitely / Probably / Maybe / Probably not)
D3. How would you prefer to receive pharmacy updates? (In-person / Phone / SMS / Printed mail)

Section E: Demographics

E1. Age range: (18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45–54 / 55–64 / 65+)
E2. Household size: (1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5+)
E3. Number of children in household: (0 / 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+)
E4. Annual household income: (Under $75K / $75K–$150K / Over $150K)
E5. How long have you lived in Prosper? (Under 1 year / 1–3 years / 3–5 years / 5+ years)

B. Competitor Observation Checklist

StarCare Pharmacy — Observation Protocol

  • Arrival: Signage visibility, entrance accessibility, waiting area comfort
  • Counter interaction: Greeting warmth, wait time, staff knowledge, pharmacist availability
  • Store layout: OTC section organization, seasonal display, promotional signage, checkout flow
  • Loyalty visibility: Is a loyalty program promoted? Where? How prominently?
  • Pricing transparency: Are prices visible? Is copay communicated proactively?
  • Departure: Follow-up information provided? Delivery options mentioned?

C. Stakeholder Interview Guide

Sample Interview Questions (Patient Version)

  • What made you choose StarCare Pharmacy over the chains nearby?
  • What would make you consider switching pharmacies?
  • How do you find out about new products or services at StarCare?
  • If StarCare had a rewards program, what would make it worth your time?
  • What does "personalized care" mean to you at a pharmacy?
  • How important is it to you that a pharmacy is involved in the Prosper community?
  • Would you refer StarCare to a friend? What would make you more likely to refer?
