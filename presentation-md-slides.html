The Game Table

Rebuilding Customer Loyalty Through Community

The Problem

More people are spending too much time on their phones.

  • 53% of consumers are trying to reduce screen time
  • Traditional retail stores are losing customers
  • Gen Z and Millennials want real social experiences
  • Frisco has no board game cafe for daytime customers

Our Solution: The Game Table

A board game cafe and retail store in Frisco, Texas.

  • 300-800 board games to play while you eat and drink
  • Food and drinks with 75-85% profit margin on coffee
  • "Third place" — not home, not school
  • Near UT Dallas (38,000+ students)

What We Found

Our research surveyed 100+ people in Frisco:

  • 68% want to spend less time on phones
  • 72% would visit a board game cafe nearby
  • 70% of students prefer used games over new
  • Competitors only open evenings, miss daytime customers

Turn the P.A.G.E. Campaign

Our strategic plan to rebuild customer loyalty:

P — Pre-Owned Game Marketplace A — App-Based Transparency G — Giving and Awareness E — Employee Engagement

P: Pre-Owned Game Marketplace

Customers sell their old games back to us.

  • Buy games at fair prices using ISBN
  • Resell at 40-55% markup
  • 70% of students prefer used games
  • Second-hand market growing 5.5% per year

A: App-Based Transparency

Mobile app for notifications and community.

  • Push notifications about new games and events
  • 90% open rate vs 20% for email
  • Customers vote on new game purchases
  • Loyalty points tracking

G: Giving and Awareness

Support local causes throughout the year.

  • Monthly cause: food bank, mental health, school clubs
  • "Awareness month" displays with themed games
  • Round-up donations at checkout
  • 40% participation rate projected

E: Employee Engagement

Happy employees = happy customers.

  • $50 weekly bonus for great service
  • Flexible scheduling and volunteer time off
  • Task rotation to prevent boredom
  • 90% employee retention target
