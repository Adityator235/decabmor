V. Strategic Plan: The Wellness Rewards Program

Pillar 2: Seva — Personalized Wellness Loyalty (Months 4–8)

With trust established through Drishti, StarCare launches its core loyalty engine: the StarCare Wellness Rewards program. This is not a points-for-purchases program. It is a points-for-health program that rewards patients for engaging in behaviors that improve their health — and for recommending StarCare to other families.

How It Works: Household Accounts

Each family creates one household account with profiles for each member (children, parents, grandparents). Every prescription filled, every health screening attended, every referral made earns "Wellness Points" for the household. Points are redeemable for OTC products, wellness services, and community benefits.

Earning Structure

Fill a prescription on time
+10 points per prescription
Complete a refills on schedule (ADAPT rate >80%)
+20 point bonus per quarter
Attend a free health screening (BP, A1C, cholesterol)
+25 points per screening
Refer a new household that fills ≥2 prescriptions
+50 points per referral
Purchase OTC products with Wellness Rewards card
+2 points per $1 spent
Complete a wellness profile update
+15 points (one-time)
Attend a StarCare wellness workshop
+30 points per session
Veterinary prescription fill
+10 points (same as human Rx)

Tier Structure: Growing With the Patient

⭐ Star 1,001+ All Tree benefits + annual wellness bundle, family health review, referral bonus multiplier (1.5×) 5% of patients

Redemption Options: Health-Focused, Not Discount-Focused

Redemption Points Cost Business Impact
$5 off OTC purchase 50 points Drives OTC traffic (28% of revenue); $5 discount on $20 purchase = 25% margin preserved
Free A1C blood glucose test 100 points Low marginal cost (~$3); drives chronic care engagement; reinforces "health partner" positioning
Free flu shot (seasonal) 75 points Low cost (~$15); drives foot traffic; seasonal engagement opportunity
Free wellness consultation (30 min) 150 points Zero marginal cost (pharmacist time); deepens relationship; cross-sell OTC
Wellness bundle (vitamins, thermometer, first aid) 300 points Wholesale cost ~$8; retail value $20; positions StarCare as health partner
DIFFERENTIATOR vs. CVS EXTRABUCKS CVS rewards spending. StarCare rewards health. CVS gives $1 back for every $50 spent. StarCare gives 25 points for attending a health screening — an activity that improves the patient's health AND increases their lifetime value. This is loyalty that makes patients healthier, not just more profitable.
