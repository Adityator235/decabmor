📖 Visit Guide & Busy Time Research

How to Measure Wait Time

Add each customer when they join the line. Tap "Served" when they get their meds. The app averages all wait times.

CVS — Typical Traffic

  • Busy: 12-1 PM, 4-6 PM Sat
  • Medium: 10-11 AM, 2-3 PM
  • Slow: 8-9 AM weekdays, 1-2 PM weekdays

CVS has drive-thru at most locations. Expect longer waits during lunch rush and Saturday morning.

H-E-B Pharmacy — Typical Traffic

  • Busy: 5-7 PM weekdays, 9-11 AM Sat
  • Medium: 3-4 PM, 12-1 PM
  • Slow: 8-9 AM weekdays, 2-4 PM weekdays

H-E-B pharmacy is inside the grocery store. Traffic spikes with grocery rush.

Prosper Pharmacy — Typical Traffic

  • Busy: 3-5 PM weekdays, 10-12 PM Sat
  • Medium: 11 AM-12 PM, 2-3 PM
  • Slow: 8-9 AM, 1-2 PM

Smaller independent — generally slower than chains. Saturday mornings busy with local regulars.

Data Points to Collect at Every Visit

  • Wait time (queue tracking)
  • Customers in store, staff on duty
  • Customer-to-staff ratio
  • Drive-thru available? Cars in line?
  • Staff interaction, name recognition, medication counseling
  • OTC display, loyalty program, digital engagement
  • Health screenings available?
  • Atmosphere, cleanliness, overall impression
