Return on Investment: Project Swastik

The total investment in Project Swastik over 12 months is $14,000 (detailed budget in Section VII). This investment generates projected revenue growth through three channels:

Revenue Channel Current (Baseline) Projected (Month 12) Incremental
Prescription revenue (increased fill rate) $93,600/yr $141,120/yr +$47,520
OTC & wellness revenue (points-driven traffic) $50,400/yr $75,600/yr +$25,200
DME & supplies revenue $21,600/yr $28,000/yr +$6,400
Veterinary revenue $14,400/yr $18,000/yr +$3,600
Total projected revenue $180,000/yr $262,720/yr +$82,720

Net projected gain: $82,720 (revenue) – $14,000 (investment) = $68,720 net Year 1 gain

Return on investment: ($68,720 / $14,000) × 100 = 491% Year 1 ROI

Break-even point: Month 3 (cumulative investment recovered through increased prescription fills and OTC traffic within first 90 days of Wellness Rewards pilot)

Scenario Analysis

Scenario Return Rate (Month 12) Net Gain ROI
Bear case (slow adoption) 60% +$22,000 157%
Base case (expected) 78% +$68,720 491%
Bull case (rapid adoption) 85% +$95,000 579%

Even in the bear case — where patient adoption is significantly slower than expected — Project Swastik delivers a 157% return. The downside is limited (most costs are staff time, not capital expenditure); the upside is substantial (60%+ revenue growth).

THE LOYALTY DIVIDEND Every dollar invested in Project Swastik returns $4.91 in Year 1. But the true value extends beyond Year 1: once a patient joins the Wellness Rewards program and builds a household account, they are unlikely to leave. The loyalty dividend compounds. By Year 2, the projected ROI exceeds 200% as the patient base stabilizes and lifetime value increases.
