# Customer Loyalty & Retention — 2026-2027 Competition Topic

## Competition Focus

**"Rebuilding Customer Loyalty and Spending"**

Collaborate with a local business or organization to:
1. **Research** what drives customer loyalty and repeat engagement
2. **Develop** a strategic plan to strengthen customer retention through:
   - Loyalty programs
   - Personalized experiences
   - Relationship-building initiatives

**Sample Company**: Combat Flip Flops (veteran-owned business)

## What Drives Customer Loyalty? (Academic Research)

A comprehensive academic study on retail customer loyalty identified these critical factors:

| Rank | Factor | Impact on Loyalty |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Merchandise Value/Quality** | Strongest driver — affects satisfaction, emotions, and repurchase intention |
| 2 | **Customer Satisfaction** | Directly leads to loyalty and repurchase |
| 3 | **Customer Experience** | Enhances emotions, perceived value, and loyalty |
| 4 | **In-Shop Emotions** | Positive emotions boost satisfaction and loyalty |
| 5 | **Perceived Value** | Trade-off between benefits received and costs paid |
| 6 | **Store Environment/Layout** | Influences customer experience and satisfaction |
| 7 | **Staff Interaction** | Professional, friendly service enhances loyalty |

## Latest Retail Loyalty Insights (2025-2026)

### University at Buffalo Study of 2,011 Shoppers

| Finding | Implication |
|---------|-------------|
| **20% discount** is the tipping point — 51% will switch retailers at this threshold | Price promotions must be carefully calibrated |
| **54% of shoppers dislike dynamic pricing** — it's a brand risk | Avoid algorithmic pricing that feels unfair |
| **Parents spend 56% more** but require deeper discounts to switch | High-value, sticky customers — target with family loyalty benefits |
| **Gen Z and Millennials** demand 50%+ discounts to switch | Younger demographics are highly price-sensitive |
| **Seniors (65+)** are most likely to switch at moderate discounts (20%) | Older customers are price-sensitive but easier to win |
| **Quality (58%) outranks price (45%)** as top driver of store choice | Merchandise quality > low prices for most shoppers |
| **Cleanliness** is rated "Very Important" by 40% of shoppers | Store environment matters more than you think |

## Loyalty Program Strategies

### 1. Tiered Loyalty Programs
```
Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum
```
- Each tier unlocks exclusive benefits
- Creates aspirational goals for customers
- Rewards high-value customers disproportionately

**Benefits per tier could include**:
- Early access to sales
- Free shipping/returns
- Birthday rewards
- Exclusive events
- Points multiplier (1.2x, 1.5x, 2x)

### 2. Personalization Engine
- Use purchase history to recommend products
- Send personalized offers based on browsing behavior
- Predict future purchases with predictive analytics
- Segment customers by behavior, not just demographics

### 3. Gamification
- Points, badges, leaderboards
- Stretch challenges (e.g., "Visit 3 times this month for bonus points")
- Milestone rewards (e.g., "Spend $500 this quarter for a special gift")
- Social sharing incentives

### 4. Multichannel Engagement
- Seamless experience across online and in-store
- Unified loyalty account across all channels
- Consistent messaging and offers everywhere
- Mobile app integration for real-time rewards

### 5. Feedback Loops
- Regular customer surveys
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) tracking
- Post-purchase reviews and ratings
- Act on feedback and communicate changes made

## Key Metrics for Loyalty Analysis

| Metric | Formula | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** | Avg Purchase Value × Purchase Frequency × Customer Lifespan | Maximize |
| **Repeat Purchase Rate** | Customers with 2+ Purchases / Total Customers | Increase |
| **Customer Retention Rate** | (End Customers - New Customers) / Start Customers | Increase |
| **Redemption Rate** | Rewards Redeemed / Rewards Earned | 30-60% optimal |
| **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** | % Promoters - % Detractors | Above 50 is excellent |
| **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** | Total Marketing Spend / New Customers | Minimize |
| **CLV:CAC Ratio** | CLV / CAC | 3:1 or higher |

## Research Methodology for Your Project

### Primary Research (Required)

#### Survey Design
- **Target**: 50-100+ respondents (customers of partner business)
- **Format**: 5-point Likert scales for perception questions
- **Sections**:
  1. Demographics (age, income, shopping frequency)
  2. Merchandise quality/value perceptions
  3. Store experience and environment ratings
  4. Staff interaction satisfaction
  5. Loyalty program awareness and effectiveness
  6. Willingness to repurchase and recommend

#### Data Collection Methods (from 1st Place Sample Entry)
- **Owner/management interviews** — deep understanding of business challenges
- **Current employee interviews** — operational insights
- **Former employee interviews** — candid perspective on gaps
- **Community survey** — broad customer sentiment
- **Focus group** — qualitative depth
- **Secret shopper analysis** — objective customer experience assessment
- **Competitor observation** — benchmarking against rivals
- **Competitor case study reviews** — learn from competitor strategies

### Secondary Research
- Industry trend reports (SAP, Adobe, Deloitte)
- Competitor loyalty program analysis
- Academic research on customer loyalty
- Market data and consumer behavior studies

## 🏆 Sample 1st Place Entry — "Turn the P.A.G.E." Campaign

Reference: [1st Place BMOR Sample Entry](10-Resources/SAMPLE-ENTRY-1-EXTRACT.txt)

### Business Context
- **Bookends**, Ridgewood, NJ — community bookstore est. 1984
- 25,000-30,000 visitors annually to downtown Ridgewood
- Hosts author and celebrity events, draws from NYC metro area
- Previous topic: CSR (structure applies to any topic)

### Research Methods Used
| Method | Purpose |
|--------|---------|
| Owner interview (Walter Boyer) | Deep business understanding |
| Current & former employee interviews | Operational gaps |
| Barnes & Noble employee interview | Competitor perspective |
| Community survey | Customer sentiment (100+ respondents) |
| Focus group | Qualitative depth |
| Secret shopper analysis | Objective experience assessment |
| Competitor observation | Benchmarking |

### Key Findings (Data-Driven)
| Finding | Data Point |
|---------|------------|
| Used books are growing | 70%+ students prefer used; market grew 5.5% in 1 year |
| Event communication is weak | Third-party reviews cite lack of transparency for last-minute changes |
| Community values social causes | 52.1% value sustainability, 66.4% value mental health, 44% value D&I |
| Employee engagement lags | Former employee noted Barnes & Noble offers better wages/benefits |

### SWOT Analysis
| Strengths | Weaknesses |
|-----------|------------|
| Small, welcoming, cherished by community | Outdated website and communication channels |
| Author/celebrity events attract regional visitors | Lack of transparency for last-minute event changes |
| Strong charitable giving, fair employee treatment | Employee engagement could improve |

| Opportunities | Threats |
|---------------|---------|
| Secondhand book market projected to grow $45B in 10 years | Barnes & Noble nearby — well-funded competitor |
| Locals interested in sustainability, mental health, D&I | Shift to online shopping (no online presence) |

### Strategic Plan: Turn the P.A.G.E. Campaign

| Letter | Initiative | Description |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **P** | **Pre-Owned Book Marketplace** | App for customers to resell books (ISBN + photo → AI pricing); online merchandise sales |
| **A** | **App-Based Transparency** | Event notifications, push alerts for last-minute changes, stakeholder inbox |
| **G** | **Giving & Awareness** | "Awareness month" book displays, round-up donations at checkout (6-month campaign) |
| **E** | **Employee Engagement** | Extended benefits, volunteer options, task rotation, weekly bonus reward system |

### KPIs (from 1st Place Entry)

| KPI | Target | How Measured |
|-----|--------|-------------|
| Third-Party Rating | Above 4.5 to 5.0 | Google Maps, TripAdvisor, Yelp feedback |
| App Adoption Rate | Above 60% | App Store feedback history |
| Donation Round-Up Participation Rate | 40% | Measured via transactions |
| Employee Retention Rate | 90% | Employee status records |
| Monthly Second-Hand Book Transactions | 150+ | Measured via the app |
| Customer & Employee Satisfaction Index | 85% | Internal satisfaction surveys |

### Budget Format (from 1st Place Entry)

| Activity | Associated Costs | % of Total | Subtotal |
|----------|-----------------|------------|----------|
| Pre-Owned Book Marketplace & App-Based Transparency (shared costs) | App development/coding, payment transaction fees, Apple App Store publication | 77.3% | $4,422 |
| Giving & Awareness Initiatives | New shelving for "awareness month" displays | 12.2% | $695 |
| Employee Engagement | $50 reward gift card to chosen employee weekly | 10.5% | $600 |
| **TOTAL** | | **100%** | **$5,717** |

### ROI Calculation (from 1st Place Entry)

```
Projected Profit:              $30,060
Total Costs:                   $5,717
ROI:                           ($30,060 - $5,717) / $5,717 × 100 = 425.80%
Breakeven Point:               April 2027 (Q2)
Amount Donated (social impact): $2,620
```

### Timeline Format (12-Month Quarterly)

| Quarter | Activities |
|---------|------------|
| **Q1** (Jan-Mar) | App coding/development, beta testing |
| **Q2** (Apr-Jun) | App rollout, beta testing stages |
| **Q3** (Jul-Sep) | Build "Awareness Month" displays, round-up donations, staff reflection |

## Loyalty Program Merchandising Integration

### How Loyalty Data Improves Buying Decisions

| Loyalty Data | Buying Decision Impact |
|--------------|----------------------|
| Purchase frequency | Adjust reorder points and safety stock |
| Product preferences | Narrow or broaden assortment |
| Price sensitivity | Optimize pricing strategy |
| Seasonal patterns | Plan seasonal inventory allocation |
| Cross-buy behavior | Create product bundles and recommendations |
| Churn signals | Target retention merchandise offers |

### Loyalty-Driven Assortment Planning
- Use purchase data to determine which products drive repeat visits
- Stock more heavily the items loyal customers buy most
- Create exclusive products for top-tier loyalty members
- Personalize product recommendations by segment

## Competition-Specific Tips

### What Judges Look For (from Official Evaluation Forms)

**Written Entry (100 pts):**
1. **Strategic Plan (36 pts)** — Actionable objectives with practical rationale, realistic timelines, effective KPIs
2. **Research Methods (16 pts)** — Clear rationale for methodology, professional execution
3. **Findings & Conclusions (16 pts)** — Data connected to objectives, evidence-based conclusions
4. **Executive Summary (10 pts)** — Clear, concise, well-structured overview
5. **Budget (8 pts)** — All costs identified, ROI addressed
6. **Introduction (8 pts)** — Business, target market, current strategy
7. **Professional Standards (6 pts)** — Layout, language, grammar

**Presentation (100 pts):**
1. **Content (75 pts)** — Business need, findings, strategic plan, budget, impact (15 pts each)
2. **Technique (20 pts)** — Organization, effectiveness, delivery, design, overall impression (5 pts each)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Using only secondary data (no primary research) ❌
- Exceeding the 20-page limit ❌
- Proposing a generic marketing plan ❌
- Neglecting the budget or timeline ❌
- Not connecting loyalty to buying/merchandising ❌
- Missing the KPIs section (12 pts alone) ❌
- Generic ROI without cost breakdown ❌

### Winning Differentiators
- **Creative initiative with a memorable framework** (e.g., "Turn the P.A.G.E." acronym)
- **Specific, measurable KPIs** with clear measurement methods
- **Detailed budget with percentages and subtotals**
- **ROI calculation with breakeven point**
- **Quarterly timeline** with specific activities per phase
- **SWOT analysis** grounded in primary research data
- **Clear connection between loyalty data and merchandise decisions**
- **Visual aids** (charts, graphs, program mockups)
